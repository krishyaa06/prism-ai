# Prism Acceptable Use Policy — India-focused draft

This draft is not legal advice. Confirm the legal operator's identity,
complaint/grievance process, and applicable Indian requirements with qualified
counsel before public launch. Contact: **prismprotocol.support@gmail.com**.

Prism is intended for tasks on devices and accounts that the user owns or is
authorized to operate. Users must:

- Obtain consent and legal authority before processing another person's data
  or controlling a device/account.
- Respect Android permission prompts, app policies, access controls, and
  system confirmation screens.
- Review plans and supervise actions that can send, publish, spend, delete,
  or change account/device state.
- Use only trusted connectors and grant least-privilege credentials.
- Avoid uploading secrets or sensitive personal data to a cloud model unless
  necessary, lawful, and explicitly intended.

Prohibited uses include unauthorized access, covert surveillance, stalking,
credential theft, fraud, spam, harassment, unlawful discrimination, evading
security controls, and actions that could cause physical harm. Prism must not
be used as the sole decision-maker for emergency, health, legal, employment,
credit, housing, or other high-impact decisions.

The service may suspend access to investigate abuse or protect users. This
draft requires legal review and an operational abuse-reporting process before
public launch.
